Bio Formatting Guide

First paragraph is shown as the short preview on the public About page card. If you add more below it, a "Read more" link opens a full page with the member's photo and the rest of the text, nicely formatted.

3 simple rules:

1. Type a line in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS to make it a section heading (e.g. ABOUT, TEACHING & COURSES).

2. Start a line with a vertical bar and a space to make it a bullet point: | PhD in Computer Science

3. Leave a blank line between paragraphs, headings, and lists.

Any email address, website, or phone number you type is automatically turned into a clickable link. No other symbols needed.

Example:

Dr. Sarah Adeyemi is a senior lecturer specializing in AI applications in education.

ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
| PhD in Computer Science, University of Lagos
| MSc in Computer Science, University of Ibadan

TEACHING & COURSES
| Introduction to AI
| Machine Learning

CONTACT
sarah.adeyemi@miu.edu.ng
+234 801 234 5678
